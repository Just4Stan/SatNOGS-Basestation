\section*{COBS Binary Protocol (RF HAT --- UART 115200 baud)}

Frame structure (little-endian): \code{[type, seq, len\_lo, len\_hi, payload\ldots, crc16\_lo, crc16\_hi]}, COBS-encoded with a 0x00 delimiter. CRC: CRC-16/CCITT-FALSE (polynomial 0x1021, init 0xFFFF). Responses use \code{type | 0x80} --- e.g. \code{PING} (0x01) response is 0x81.

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{llX}
        \toprule
        Type & Name & Description \\
        \midrule
        0x01 & \code{PING} & Connectivity test \\
        0x02 & \code{GET\_INFO} & CC1200 part/version \\
        0x03 & \code{SET\_STATE} & Strobe CC1200 state machine \\
        0x04 & \code{SET\_STREAMING} & Enable/disable continuous RX forwarding \\
        0x05 & \code{GET\_METRICS} & RSSI, MARC state, FIFO levels \\
        0x10 & \code{READ\_REG} & Normal register read \\
        0x11 & \code{WRITE\_REG} & Normal register write \\
        0x12 & \code{READ\_EXT} & Extended register read \\
        0x13 & \code{WRITE\_EXT} & Extended register write \\
        0x20 & \code{RX\_READ} & Read bytes from RX FIFO \\
        0x21 & \code{STROBE} & Issue raw CC1200 strobe command \\
        0x22 & \code{TX\_WRITE} & Write to TX FIFO \\
        0x23 & \code{TX\_SEND} & Strobe TX \\
        0x24 & \code{TX\_FLUSH} & Flush TX FIFO \\
        0x30 & \code{PROFILE\_CLEAR} & Clear loaded profile buffer \\
        0x31 & \code{PROFILE\_BEGIN} & Start SmartRF profile load \\
        0x32 & \code{PROFILE\_CHUNK} & Profile data chunk \\
        0x33 & \code{PROFILE\_APPLY} & Apply loaded profile \\
        0x34 & \code{SET\_FREQ\_WORD} & Atomic Doppler frequency update \\
        0x35 & \code{SET\_SERIAL\_MODE} & Raw serial / AX.25 G3RUH PIO mode \\
        0x40 & \code{SELECT\_RADIO} & Switch UHF/VHF \\
        0x50 & \code{BUZZER} & Play pattern (0x01--0x08) \\
        0xE0 & \code{EVT\_RX\_DATA} & Received packet (async, firmware$\to$Pi) \\
        0xE1 & \code{EVT\_ERROR} & Error event (async, firmware$\to$Pi) \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabularx}
    \caption{COBS binary protocol message types (RF HAT $\leftrightarrow$ Pi).}
\end{table}
